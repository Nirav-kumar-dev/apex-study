# ⚡ Apex Study OS — Release v1.0.0

> **The Ultimate AI-Powered Study Operating System for CBSE Students (Classes 7th to 10th)**

Apex Study OS is a high-performance desktop and cloud study environment designed to help students master their syllabus, track exam preparation, practice interactive mock tests, and learn with an AI Tutor.

---

## 🚀 What's New in v1.0.0

### 💻 1. Standalone Windows Desktop App & Installer
* **`apex-study-installer.exe`**: One-click setup wizard that installs the entire suite safely into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\ApexStudy` *(no administrator privileges required)*.
* **`apex.exe`**: High-performance, GPU-accelerated desktop window with custom icon, distraction-free view, and offline textbook engine.
* **Desktop & Start Menu Shortcuts**: Instant access right from your Windows desktop and Start menu.
* **Automatic Cleanup Watchdog**: Built-in self-cleanup and uninstaller that completely wipes app data and shortcuts when uninstalled.

### ☁️ 2. Firebase Cloud Account & Realtime Sync
* **Google Authentication**: Seamless one-tap login with Google.
* **Realtime Multi-Device Sync**: Any progress made (study timers, completed tasks, exam scores, notes) syncs instantly across all your devices via Firebase Realtime Database.
* **Strict Security Rules**: Zero public access — all student data is protected with authenticated owner-only access rules.

### 🤖 3. NVIDIA NIM AI Tutor & Reasoning Engine
* **Interactive AI Doubt Solver**: Socratic reasoning and step-by-step explanations tailored to the CBSE curriculum.
* **AI Mock Exam Generator**: Automatically creates customized question papers (MCQs, short answer, numericals, proofs) with instant AI grading.

### 📚 4. Embedded NCERT Textbook Library
* **Full Curriculum Books**: Built-in chapter-wise reader for Mathematics, Science, Social Science, and English.
* **Integrated Notes & Formulas**: Key formulas, revision checklists, and page-range references linked directly to every chapter.

### 🧠 5. Spaced-Repetition Revision & Error Tracker
* **1-3-7-14-30 Day Spaced Repetition**: Automatic algorithm that schedules revision items right before you forget them.
* **Intelligent Error Log**: Track mistakes by category (*Conceptual*, *Calculation*, *Formula*, *Careless*) with remediation notes.

---

## 📥 Installation Instructions

1. Download **`apex-study-installer.exe`** from the **Assets** section below.
2. Double-click the installer file and click **Install Now**.
3. Launch **Apex Study OS** from your Desktop shortcut!

---

## 🛠️ System Requirements
* **OS**: Windows 10 / Windows 11 (64-bit)
* **RAM**: 2 GB minimum (4 GB recommended)
* **Disk Space**: ~1 GB (includes complete embedded textbook library)
